Cube Icons

Pixel drawings built from cubes that fly in, settle and fly apart. Point at an icon to build it, and shape it with everything on the right: start from a preset, then change the look, colour, depth and motion. The code under the controls always matches what you see. New here? Get started takes two minutes. Draw your own icons, or turn an image into one, in the editor, and see twenty-eight ways to use them in the recipes.

    Scroll assembly
    
    
    Each object builds as its section reaches the middle of the screen and comes apart as it leaves. Scrub ties the build to the scroll, cube by cube from the bottom row up; Trigger flies the whole icon in once half of it is in view. One line each: CubeIcons.scroll(stage, "rocket").
