Privacy

Cube Icons doesn't collect personal data. This page covers the library, the website and the AI skill.

The library

cubes.js and icons.js run entirely in the visitor's browser. They make no network requests, set no cookies and store nothing.

The website

cube-icons.attwa.me counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics: page views, referrers, countries and device types, aggregated and anonymous. It uses no cookies and doesn't identify or track people across sites.

Icons you save in the editor's "My icons" stay in your own browser's storage and are never sent anywhere.

The AI skill

The Cube Icons skill is a set of instructions and two script files that your AI assistant reads. It collects nothing, sends nothing and reads no credentials. Your conversations stay with the assistant you use, under that provider's own privacy policy.
