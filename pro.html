Questions

Will the free version stay free?

Yes. The engine, the icons, the tools and the Claude skill are MIT-licensed and stay that way, including for commercial work. Pro adds to them; it doesn't take anything away.

Why one payment and not a subscription?

Nothing in Cube Icons costs anything to run: no servers, no AI credits. So there's nothing to bill monthly. Pay once and keep what you bought.

Can I build these sections myself with the free version?

Yes, the engine is the same. Pro saves you the design and build time: finished, responsive sections, tuned and ready to paste.

Do I need Pro to use Cube Icons with Claude?

No. The free skill covers the engine, the icon format and the recipes. Claude can draw new icons for you with it.

When does Pro launch, and what will it cost?

Pricing and the date come with the launch. Follow the GitHub repo to hear first.
